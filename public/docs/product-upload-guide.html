Product Upload Workbook

SEO · AEO · GEO

A reusable field-by-field guide for real product uploads, supplier verification, bilingual content, images, publication and search readiness.

Use for: Unbranded/loose goods, supplier-branded goods and future verified 2TBR private-label products across Direct Purchase, Community Pools and Group Deals.

100% means complete readiness checks, not search rankings.
Google/AI search inclusion, citation, merchant eligibility and sales cannot be guaranteed.

Five-step workflow

  1. Verify the actual item, selling unit, brand and genuine product photograph.
  2. Create or select an existing SKU in Admin → Products.
  3. Complete the product's SEO/AEO/GEO editor in both languages.
  4. Save as Draft, then submit to a different Super Admin for review.
  5. Check approved EN/BN pages, canonical URLs and sitemap before use.

Correct media roles prevent campaign artwork from being confused with the genuine product. Accurate accessible alt text improves usability and describes the actual photograph without keyword repetition.
